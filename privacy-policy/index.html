Privacy Policy

Mercia

Last updated: February 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Mercia ("we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, and handles your information when you use our mobile application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our practices.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information when you use Mercia:

  • Account information – email address and authentication credentials used to create and manage your account.
  • Usage data – interactions within the app, such as messages, session activity, and feature usage, to provide and improve the service.
  • Device information – device type, operating system version, and app version for diagnostic purposes.
  • Subscription and purchase data – transaction records associated with in-app purchases or subscriptions.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the Mercia experience.
  • Authenticate users and manage accounts securely.
  • Process subscriptions and in-app purchases.
  • Respond to support requests and communications.
  • Analyze usage patterns to enhance app features and performance.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, a cloud database platform. Data is encrypted in transit using TLS and at rest using industry-standard encryption. We retain your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you with the service. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

4. Third-Party Services

Mercia integrates with the following third-party services, each of which may process certain data as described below:

Supabase

Used for secure user authentication and cloud data storage. Supabase processes your account credentials and app data on our behalf. For more information, see the Supabase Privacy Policy.

RevenueCat

Used to manage in-app subscriptions and purchases. RevenueCat processes transaction and subscription data to enable and validate your purchases. For more information, see the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

Groq AI

Used to power AI-driven features within the app. When you interact with AI features, your inputs may be sent to Groq's API for processing. We do not store or share these inputs beyond what is necessary to generate a response. For more information, see the Groq Privacy Policy.

5. User Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access – request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction – request that we correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion – request that we delete your account and associated data.
  • Portability – request a machine-readable export of your data.
  • Opt-out – opt out of any non-essential data processing.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at the email address below.
